# Code of conduct

CaseKeeper exists for people going through family court, often at a painful time. Everyone who
takes part here is expected to keep that in mind.

## Expected

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to people who are new or upset.
- Assume good faith, and keep disagreements about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy: never ask for, share or speculate about anyone's case or personal details.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Posting anyone's personal information, case details or documents.
- Taking sides in, or commenting on, anyone's real dispute.
- Giving or soliciting legal advice as if it were authoritative.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, or block people who break these rules.
Report problems privately through <https://darkciphersystems.com/report.html>. Reports are dealt
with in confidence.
