About CaseKeeper

CaseKeeper helps you organise documents for family court proceedings in England & Wales.
It keeps everything on this computer: documents, notes, your timeline and complaint records
are stored locally and are never uploaded anywhere.

Before you continue, please note:

1. CaseKeeper is a filing and organising tool. It does not give legal advice, and nothing it
   produces is a substitute for advice from a solicitor, barrister or advice service.

2. Bundles it produces follow the general shape of Practice Direction 27A, but you are
   responsible for checking that anything you file meets the directions your court has given.

3. Documents and information from family proceedings about children are usually confidential.
   Sharing them with people who are not parties can be contempt of court unless the Family
   Procedure Rules allow it or the court gives permission. Check before you share.

4. The built-in guide and the optional AI tools give general information only. AI output can be
   wrong: always check it against your documents. The AI is off until you turn it on, and then
   only talks to the Ollama server you choose (normally your own computer).

5. The software is provided as-is, with no warranty. Please keep your own backups; CaseKeeper
   has a built-in backup feature under Settings, and keeps daily restore points automatically.

6. Upgrading over an existing installation keeps all of your existing data.
